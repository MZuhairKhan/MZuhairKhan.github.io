A submission to the **Quantum Creative Winter Fest** (2023), made with [Rihab Hoceini](https://github.com/HoceiniRihab) and [Rishi Koushik T](https://github.com/RishiKoushik). We wrote a short space-exploration story and threaded quantum-computing puzzles through it, so a reader moves the narrative forward by solving Qiskit challenges in the accompanying `The_Third_Kind.ipynb` notebook and picks up the concepts by doing rather than reading. The repository holds the story (as a PDF and slides) and the challenge notebook, along with a bonus problem carried over from the IIITDWD QCC Hackathon.
